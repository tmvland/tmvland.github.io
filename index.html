# tmvland.github.io

Hello! I am Theo Landman, and I am a Assistant Specialist in the department of Neuroimmunology at the University of California, San Francisco. I hold a Master's degree in Biomedical Imaging from UCSF as well as a Bachelor's degree from the University of Michigan in mathematics, physics, and complex systems. My primary research interest is in the development of numerical methods for solving physical problems; right now most specifically this means developing image processing pipelines for MRI and CT among other imaging modalities. I'm interested in the application of math to scientific computing and physical modeling. I love differential equations, and will talk about them at pretty much any available opportunity. Right now I'm mainly working on developing processing pipelines related to imaging for multiple sclerosis. My most recent completed project is on developing quality assurance metrics using a litany of edge-detection methods for anatomically accurate synthetic CT. Stuff related to that might crop up on here-- or other image reconstruction codes I want to keep hold of for later. My resume can be found here.
